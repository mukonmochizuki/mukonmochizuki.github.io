Website Standard Operating Procedures (Japanese Site)

Follow these step-by-step workflows to update the Japanese side of your live website.

SCENARIO A: Finished an event (and nothing new to announce yet)

Step 1: Update the Homepage (index-jp.html)

Action: In index-jp.html, scroll down to <h2 class="section-title">次回の公演</h2>.
Delete the entire <div class="featured-event-grid"> ... </div> block.
Paste the "Coming Soon" code below in its place. 

<!-- COMING SOON PLACEHOLDER (JAPANESE) -->
<div style="border: 1px dashed rgba(232, 224, 212, 0.2); background-color: var(--warm-dark); padding: 5rem 2rem; text-align: center; margin-bottom: 2rem;">
  <h3 style="font-size: 1.6rem; font-weight: 300; letter-spacing: 0.1em; color: var(--off-white); margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: 'Noto Serif JP', serif;">次回の公演をお楽しみに</h3>
  <p style="color: var(--muted); margin-bottom: 2.5rem; font-size: 1.05rem;">新しい公演情報が決定次第、こちらでお知らせいたします。</p>
  <div class="button-group">
    <a href="events-jp.html" class="card-btn secondary">過去の公演情報を見る</a>
  </div>
</div>

Step 2: Clean up the Events Page (events-jp.html)

Action: In events-jp.html, look above the <h2 class="section-label" id="past-performances">過去の公演実績</h2> header.
Delete the entire <div class="event-section"> ... </div> that holds the old upcoming event. Leave nothing above the Past Performances header.

Step 3: Add to Past Archive (events-jp.html)

Action: In events-jp.html, find <div class="past-events-grid">.
Paste this thumbnail code right underneath it, so it becomes the newest item in the grid. 

<div class="past-event-thumb" 
     data-img="YOUR_FLYER_IMAGE.jpg" 
     data-title="公演名（フルタイトル）" 
     data-date="公演日" 
     data-venue="会場名" 
     data-role="役割（出演、大鼓など）" 
     data-desc="公演の概要や説明をここに記載します。">
  <img src="YOUR_FLYER_IMAGE.jpg" alt="チラシ画像の代替テキスト" />
  <div class="thumb-overlay">
    <span>YEAR</span>
    <h4>短い<br>タイトル</h4>
  </div>
</div>

SCENARIO B: Have a NEW upcoming performance to publicize?

Step 1: Update the Homepage (index-jp.html)

Action: In index-jp.html, scroll down to <h2 class="section-title">次回の公演</h2>.
Delete the "Coming Soon" placeholder block.
Paste this Event Grid code in its place and fill in the details. 

<div class="featured-event-grid">
  <img class="event-chirashi" src="YOUR_FLYER_IMAGE.jpg" alt="公演名" />
  <div class="event-details">
    <span class="event-date">公演日</span>
    <p class="event-info"><strong>公演名</strong><br>時間<br><a href="MAP_LINK" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: var(--orange-rope); text-decoration: underline; text-underline-offset: 4px;">会場名</a></p>
    <div class="button-group">
      <a href="YOUR_TICKET_LINK" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="card-btn">チケットを購入</a>
      <a href="events-jp.html" class="card-btn secondary">すべての公演を見る</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

Step 2: Update the Events Page (events-jp.html)

Action: In events-jp.html, paste this full event section directly above the <h2 class="section-label" id="past-performances">過去の公演実績</h2> tag. 

<div class="event-section">
  <div class="event-grid">
    <img class="event-chirashi" src="YOUR_FLYER_IMAGE.jpg" alt="公演チラシ" />
    <div class="event-details">
      <span class="event-date">公演日</span>
      <h3 class="event-title">公演名</h3>
      <div class="event-info">
        <p><strong>時間:</strong> 時間をここに記載</p>
        <p><strong>場所:</strong> <a href="MAP_LINK" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: var(--orange-rope); text-decoration: underline; text-underline-offset: 4px;">会場名</a></p>
        <br>
        <p>公演の概要や説明をここに記載します。</p>
      </div>
      <div class="button-group">
        <a href="YOUR_TICKET_LINK" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="card-btn">チケットを購入</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
